Overview

In the parish of Misericórdia, on a quiet street between the Chiado and the river. The palace is a short walk from the Tagus, with the city’s oldest quarters on every side.

  • Grand reception rooms.
  • Ornate ceilings.
  • Gardens, terraces and views of the Tagus.
